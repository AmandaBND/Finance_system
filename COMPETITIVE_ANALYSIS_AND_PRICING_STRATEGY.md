**Stripe Billing** (Payment-Focused)
- Pay-as-you-go: 0.5% + $0.30 per transaction
- Custom enterprise: Negotiated pricing
- NO subscription model (transaction-based)

#### COMMON PRICING STRATEGIES

1. **Per-User Model** (Most Common)
   - Used by: QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks
   - Avg cost: $15-25 per additional user
   - Pros: Scales with team size
   - Cons: Discourages many users

2. **Flat-Rate Model**
   - Used by: Wave (free), Zoho (partially)
   - Pros: Simple, unlimited users
   - Cons: Less revenue growth

3. **Feature-Tiered Model**
   - Base features in free/starter tier
   - Advanced features gated in higher tiers
   - Most common approach

4. **Usage-Based Model**
   - Becoming more popular
   - Stripe Billing example
   - Based on: invoices, transactions, storage
   - Pros: Fair pricing, aligns value
   - Cons: Unpredictable costs

5. **Hybrid Models**
   - Base price + per-user seats
   - Base price + usage overage
   - Most companies moving here

#### INDUSTRY BENCHMARKS

- **59% of accounting software buyers budget ≤$100/user/month**
- **Entry-level typical: $40/user/month**
- **Advanced/Mid-market: $255/user/month** (Capterra data)
- **Free tier users: 15-25% conversion to paid**
- **Annual billing discount: 15-25% typical**

## 4. PRICING STRATEGY RECOMMENDATIONS

### Positioning

**Starter Tier Strategy:**
- Keep FREE tier forever to acquire customers
- Emphasize "Free accounting for life" vs Wave's inconsistency
- Maximum 10 invoices/month limit to encourage upgrade
- No ads (unlike some competitors)
- Goal: 20-25% conversion to Professional within 6 months

**Professional Tier Strategy:**
- **Sweet spot for SMBs** ($35 base makes it affordable; $8/user seat competitive vs QB $15-20)
- Focus marketing: "Invoicing + Payroll in One Place" (unique vs competitors)
- Includes all basic needs for small team
- Goal: Highest volume tier (60-70% of paying users)

**Business Tier Strategy:**
- Position as "growing company" tier (6-20 employees)
- Emphasize time savings: AI expense categorization, forecasting, advanced reporting
- Project accounting is differentiator vs entry-level competitors
- Goal: 20-25% of paying users

**Enterprise Tier Strategy:**
- Custom pricing for deals $200-1,000+/month
- Focus on compliance, security, unlimited scale
- White-label and integration options
- Dedicated support critical
- Goal: 5-10% of customers generating 40-50% of revenue

### Revenue Optimization

1. **Annual Billing Discount:** 15-20% (vs 23% monthly)
   - Professional: $35 → $29/month ($348/year)
   - Business: $75 → $62/month ($744/year)
   - Enterprise: Custom negotiation

2. **Add-On Pricing (Optional - Advanced)**
   - Priority support: $50/month
   - Advanced integrations: $75-200/month per integration
   - White-label: +$200-500/month
   - Custom development: Hourly billing

3. **Expansion Revenue Tactics**
   - Seat expansion: Easy seat addition (no contract changes)
   - Feature upgrades: Move free → Professional at point of need
   - Usage-based overages (optional): Invoice overage if client exceeds limits (5-10% revenue)

4. **Acquisition Funnels**
   - Free tier → Professional: 20-25% conversion target in 6 months
   - Professional → Business: 10-15% expansion rate annually
   - Business → Enterprise: High-touch sales, 20% expansion rate

### vs QuickBooks ($30-235/month)

- **YOUR ADVANTAGE:** Simpler UX, better employee/client portals, better support
- **THEIR ADVANTAGE:** Lower cost, more features in free tier
- **Strategy:** Position as "Zoho simplified" with better UX

### vs Wave (FREE)
- **YOUR ADVANTAGE:** Free tier has payroll, better mobile, superior UX
- **THEIR ADVANTAGE:** Core product is free
- **Strategy:** Offer free tier with payroll basics as loss-leader; upsell to Professional

### vs Xero ($25-105/month)
- **YOUR ADVANTAGE:** Lower entry point, payroll included, better UX
